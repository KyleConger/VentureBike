# Competitor Intelligence Strategy — Room for Growth

**Team research priority:** Maximize learning about **classmates (competitors)** to see where growth is still available.

## Q1 reality check

| Phase | What we can know | What we do |
|-------|------------------|------------|
| **Q1 (now)** | Same starting line; free end-user survey; no rival sales yet | Map market structure; hypothesize where peers will crowd; design CI system; prioritize paid reports for later |
| **Q2+** | Test-market results, brand/ad judgments, prices, cities, capacity signals | Buy research aggressively where it reveals peer gaps; attack underserved demand |

“Room for growth” ≠ only largest segment. It is **demand we can capture because peers are weak, absent, mistargeted, or capacity-constrained**.

## Growth-room framework (use every quarter)

For each segment × city (and web):

1. **Attractiveness** — potential, WTP, need clarity  
2. **Crowding** — how many classmates likely / actually competing well  
3. **Serveability** — can we design, staff, and supply it (RBV resources)?  
4. **Gap score** — high attractiveness + low effective crowding + we can serve = **priority growth room**

## Hypotheses to test (edit after survey paste)

Classmates often:

- [ ] Over-focus on one “favorite” segment and leave another thin  
- [ ] Open the same obvious first city → fight on share while another city is softer  
- [ ] Mis-map features to benefits (Brand Judgment gaps)  
- [ ] Under-advertise or mismatch ads (Ad Judgment gaps) — Q2+  
- [ ] Underpay staff → productivity drag — Q2+  
- [ ] Undersize capacity → unserved demand (Market Performance: share × % demand served)

Our counter-posture (aligned with personal goals, still not locked):

- Presence in **all three segments** → harder for peers to own a whole category  
- **Physical + web** → catch demand peers leave online or offline  
- **City cadence to all four by Q4** → deny geographic monopoly  
- **Ad parity + pay above average** → close easy judgment/productivity gaps  
- **Analytics** → buy and use competitor-facing research when available  

## Purchased research — maximize competitor signal

When the Workspace offers buyable studies, prioritize in this order **unless cash floor forbids**:

| Priority | Research type (names vary by sim build) | Why for growth room |
|----------|-----------------------------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | Competitor brands / features / brand judgment | See who failed segment fit |
| 2 | Competitor prices / rebates / price judgments | Find under/over pricing gaps |
| 3 | Competitor ads / media / ad judgment | Match or beat top ads (team goal) |
| 4 | Competitor sales outlets, web, staffing | See unserved cities/channels |
| 5 | Market share / demand served by firm | Quantify leftover demand |
| 6 | Broader industry / satisfaction studies | Context only after CI essentials |

**Cash rule:** never buy research that breaks **Cash + CD ≥ $300,000** in Q1. In later quarters, weigh research ROI vs ads/capacity—but keep the **competitor-first** purchase bias.
