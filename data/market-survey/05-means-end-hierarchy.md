# Means-End Hierarchy — Brand Design Aid

Customers buy **benefits** that serve **end states**, delivered by **attributes**.  
Speculative only until the team locks brands in `quarters/Q1/decisions.md`.

```
Desired end states (goals / values)
        ↑
Consequences (benefits / costs / outcomes)
        ↑
Attributes (features, parts, services)
```
