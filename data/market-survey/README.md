# Q1 Market Research — Guide

**Primary research focus:** Maximize insight on **competitors (classmates)** to find **room for growth**.  
Use free Workspace survey data first to map the market; treat classmate white space as the growth thesis.

**Rule:** Paste numbers from the sim. Do not invent scores.

## Why competitor focus in Q1?

In Q1 everyone starts with the same resources and **no sales results yet**. “Competitor research” now means:

1. **Predict crowding** — which segments/cities classmates are most likely to fight over  
2. **Find underserved value** — needs/applications ≥110 that are easy for rivals to under-deliver  
3. **Build the CI system** — templates + purchase plan so Q2+ reports convert into growth moves  
4. **RBV** — superior **business analytics** (what we track and buy) becomes a scarce capability vs peers

Room for growth = demand we can serve **better or earlier** than classmates (design fit, city timing, channel, later ads/price)—not just “big potential” in isolation.

## Reading the numbers (sim tips)

- Sort needs (and applications) **by importance within each segment**.  
- Customers buy **benefits**, not parts — map attributes → consequences → end states (means-end).  
- WTP is a **maximum for the ideal brand**; above it, fewer buyers enter. Lower prices please customers but may or may not maximize revenue (elasticity).  
- Market potential = `P(purchase) × units × # potential customers` (researcher-derived).  
- Do **not** assume 25% of annual potential per quarter early on — weak early marketing, learning lag, skepticism. Actual early demand is usually **much lower**; later it can catch up or exceed naive splits if execution is strong.  
- Intentions are rough; results depend on design, price, ads, distribution, and **how well you and competitors serve the market**.

## MOA checklist (class)

A proper Market Opportunity Analysis organizes:

- [ ] Macro forces shaping the opportunity (tech: 3D CF; intro PLC; multi-firm entry)  
- [ ] Market structure & where we fit (3 segments × 4 cities × web/physical)  
- [ ] Minimum requirements to serve (end-user profiles + **competitor** profiles as they emerge)
