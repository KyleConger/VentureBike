# Venture Bike Simulation — Canonical Brief

Source: MAN6724 course materials / Marketplace Venture Strategy (Bikes). Stored for team reuse.

## The opportunity

License technology to build **carbon fiber bikes** via **3D printing** and a new economical carbon fiber. Parts can be made globally; material is stronger, lighter, and now cheaper relative to light metals.

### New bike shop model

- Traditional shops: 50–100 bikes on display (gender × model × sizes).
- 3D printing: **one display copy per model**; smaller, less crowded outlets; space used for **custom fitting**.

### New price point

- Traditional carbon fiber: very expensive / elite only.
- New process: price well below classic carbon fiber; competes with **higher-end metal bikes**.

### Target customer

- Higher end of the **conventional** bike market: affluent enough to stretch for “elite” carbon at a barely-affordable price; status + ride quality drive purchase.
- Profitability of this niche must be proven; lower price / more value may pull additional riders.

## Consumer advantages

- Lighter → easier to pedal
- Carbon dampens vibration → more comfort
- Custom frame to gender, size, shape, weight, performance → less fatigue
- More affordable than classic carbon
- Access to “latest/greatest” look and status

## Business advantages

- Streamlined production; less equipment; warehouse can be a fab site
- ~1 frame/hour via printer vs long skilled labor cycles
- Lower material cost (new carbon from low-cost polyester)
- **No finished-goods inventory** — build to order
- Lower debt/equity tied up in inventory
- Smaller, cheaper, more aesthetic shops
- Faster perfect fit → fewer lost sales

## Company model

- Fully integrated: marketing → production → HR; limited capital; full accounting responsibility
- Seed capital from executive team + later VCs
- **Six quarters** to become self-sufficient and earn substantial profits
- Goal: **best competitor** on the balanced scorecard

## Success measurement (balanced scorecard)

Financial performance · Marketing effectiveness · Market performance · Investment in the future · Human resource management · Manufacturing productivity · Asset management · Financial risk · Creation of wealth

## Competitive setting

- Four test markets: **New York, Rio de Janeiro, Amsterdam, Bangalore**
- Carbon fiber niche historically elite-priced; industry entering **introductory PLC stage** with moderate pricing
- Multiple firms enter simultaneously with equal starting resources
- **Company-owned outlets only** (no third-party bike stores in this test)
- Optional **web sales center** as digital channel

## Segments

| Segment | Needs | Price stance |
|---------|--------|--------------|
| **Recreation** | Fun, light exercise, social; comfort & easy handling; durable for paths/light terrain; carry small items | Moderately price sensitive; pays for comfort/ease, not much more |
| **Mountain** | Off-road adventure/sport; sturdy, powerful, agile | Willing to pay premium for terrain capability |
| **Speed** | Lightest, fastest, most aerodynamic; racing/group rides | High willingness to pay; will sacrifice comfort/utility |
