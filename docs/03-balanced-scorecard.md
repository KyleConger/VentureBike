# Balanced Scorecard — Decision Checklist

Use before locking each quarter. Full class formulas: `05-class-framework-rbv-bsc.md`.

| Pillar | Class formula (summary) | Standing tactics |
|--------|-------------------------|------------------|
| Financial performance | ((Net profit from current ops + Gross profit) / 2) / Shares issued | Contribution margins; avoid emergency financing |
| Marketing effectiveness | (Avg brand judgment/100 + Avg ad judgment/100) / 2 | Segment-true designs; **match top ads** |
| Investment in future | (Cum. future-benefit expenses / Cum. net revenues) × 10 + 1 | Capacity, R&D, systems that pay forward |
| Wealth | Net equity / Total stockholders equity | Sustainable equity value |
| Manufacturing productivity | (Pct capacity used)/100 − (Overtime pct of capacity used)/(2×100) | High utilization without heavy overtime |
| Human resources | (Sales productivity/100 + Production productivity/100) / 2 | Comp **> average** |
| Market performance | (Avg share in targeted segments/100) × (Pct demand served/100) | All target segments; serve demand (capacity + staff) |

Other Marketplace pillars (e.g., asset management, financial risk) may still appear in-sim—watch the live scorecard.

**Q1 cash rule:** Cash + CD ≥ **$300,000**.

## Team goal ↔ BSC map

- All segments → Market performance  
- Digital + physical → Marketing / market coverage  
- Max spend + emergency cash → Investment in future ↔ financial discipline (Cash+CD floor)  
- Top ads → Marketing effectiveness (Ad Judgment)  
- Cities by Q4 → Market performance / investment in future  
- Comp > average → HR (productivity)
