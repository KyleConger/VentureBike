## Strategic intent

Become the balanced-scorecard leader by owning the **affordable custom carbon** positioning across **Recreation, Mountain, and Speed**, with **omnichannel** reach (web + all four cities by Q4), **above-average people investment**, and **ad parity or better**.

## Positioning

- Promise: elite carbon performance and custom fit at a price that stretches but does not exclude the high-end conventional buyer.  
- Proof: segment-true designs, fitting-led shops, reliable delivery, strong ads.
