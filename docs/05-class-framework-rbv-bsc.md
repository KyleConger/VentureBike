# Class Framework — RBV, System Rules & BSC

Instructor / course emphasis for MAN6724 Venture Bike. Complements `00-simulation-brief.md`.

## Strategic lens: Resource-Based View (RBV)

This class uses the **resource-based view** of strategic planning. Advantage comes from building and combining **valuable, scarce, hard-to-imitate resources and capabilities** inside the firm—not only from picking an attractive industry position.

### How RBV maps to this venture

| Resource / capability | Simulation expression |
|----------------------|------------------------|
| Licensed tech | Carbon fiber + 3D printing process (starting endowment shared by all firms) |
| Brands & designs | Segment-fit products → Brand Judgment |
| Go-to-market assets | Stores, web center, sales force, ad equity → Ad Judgment & demand served |
| Operations system | Factory, fixed/operating capacity, worker productivity |
| Human capital | Compensation, sales & production productivity |
| Financial buffer | Cash + CD; avoidance of emergency loans / excess leverage |
| Analytics | Using free + purchased market info better than rivals |
| Leadership / integration | Coherent choices across all tactical areas |

**Implication:** every quarter, ask “which resources are we building?” and “do our tactics strengthen those resources under sim rules?” External blog tips matter less than **closed-system data** and **BSC formulas**.

## Balanced Scorecard — formulas (class)

Use these when evaluating tradeoffs. (Asset management / financial risk may also appear in the full Marketplace BSC; weight what the live scorecard shows.)

### Financial Performance

\[
\frac{(\text{Net Profit from Current Operations} + \text{Gross Profit}) / 2}{\text{Total Shares Issued}}
\]

### Marketing Effectiveness

\[
\frac{\text{Average Brand Judgment}/100 + \text{Average Ad Judgment}/100}{2}
\]

### Investment in Future

\[
\left(\frac{\text{Cumulative Expenses that Benefit Firm's Future}}{\text{Cumulative Net Revenues}}\right) \times 10 + 1
\]

### Wealth

\[
\frac{\text{Net Equity}}{\text{Total Stockholders Equity}}
\]

### Manufacturing Productivity

\[
\frac{\text{Percent of Operating Capacity Used in Production}}{100}
- \frac{\text{Overtime as Percent of Operating Capacity Used}}{2 \times 100}
\]

### Human Resource Management

\[
\frac{\text{Sales Force Productivity}/100 + \text{Production Worker Productivity}/100}{2}
\]

### Market Performance

\[
\left(\frac{\text{Average Market Share in Targeted Segments}}{100}\right)
\times
\left(\frac{\text{Percent of Demand Actually Served}}{100}\right)
\]

### Q1 relevance of formulas

- Q1 has **no sales yet** — Brand Judgment foundation is set by **design quality**; Ad Judgment starts in Q2.  
- Factory sizing starts **Investment in Future** and later **Manufacturing Productivity**.  
- Cash/CD discipline protects later **Financial** and risk-related scores.  
- Targeting all three segments sets up **Market Performance** once demand is served.
