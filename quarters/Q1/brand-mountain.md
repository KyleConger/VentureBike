## Design logic

- Capability stack matches Mountain must-dos: rough terrain, incline, soften impact, stop quickly, shifting.
- All-purpose seat (not comfort) avoids soft-ride / comfort cautions.
- Mountain tires (not hybrid) avoid on+off-road caution.
- Decals added for Colorful / Stylish Important needs without going “sleek race.”
- No reflectors: style/rugged preference over visibility kit (visibility only 103).
