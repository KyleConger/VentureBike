## 6. Channel insight (physical + digital)

**Data says customers want both digital ease and human help.**

| Signal | Implication |
|--------|-------------|
| High “likes to buy on the web” (all segments) | Web sales center is demand-relevant, not just “nice to have” |
| Higher “wants human sales reps/support” (esp. Rec 133) | Physical outlets / staffed selling still critical |
| Credit security tops Rec needs (135) | Trust/UX on web affects conversion |
| Repair nearby Important all segments | Local presence / service story supports purchase confidence |

Aligns with instructor guidance (online-only limits growth) and team goal (digital + physical).

> **Human call — web + store in Q1**  
> Spreadsheet supports **both**, but cash, staffing later, and Cash+CD ≥ $300k constrain timing/scale.  
> *Questions:* Open web + city #1 in Q1? How minimal can web be at open while still scoring on “easy/secure”? How do we staff humans in Q2 without starving production pay (comp > average)?
