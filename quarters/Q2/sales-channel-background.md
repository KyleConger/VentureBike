### Sales force size & training

- All staff can sell/service **all brands**, but you may **specialize** (extra training → more proficiency + better customer perception of know-how).  
- Example: 5 people → 1 service · 3 primary segment · 1 secondary.  
- After-sale **service matters** (flats, tune-ups, care/maintenance advice).  
- Training assignments **need not sum to headcount** — unassigned people sell to **all segments equally**.

**Test-market tip:** hire **no more than 7** sales/service people **per city**; assign **≥1 to service**.

Sim guideline (Q1 notes): productivity risk if **>~15** display bikes or **>~15** salespeople in one store — test-market tip (≤7) is well under that.

### Demand forecast caution

- 12-month potential ≠ quarterly demand. Naive “÷4” (25%/quarter) is **unlikely** early.  
- Early PLC: weak marketing/products industry-wide + slow customer learning/skepticism → **actual << potential**. Later, demand can exceed naive quarterly shares.  
- Estimates are rough; buying intent ≠ purchases. Realized demand depends on design, price, ads, distribution, and competitive service.
