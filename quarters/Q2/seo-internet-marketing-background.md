## What “Internet marketing” covers (sim framing)

Uses the Internet to deliver promotional messages. Includes SEM, social, display/banners, email, mobile.  
**This quarter:** start by creating a **marketing website** with content that appeals to target segments. Full internet campaign tools unlock over later quarters.

## Web page content

- Convert ads designed for media into web pages (same messages, web layout).
- Preview ads in **web page format** from Ad Design.
- Workspace lists ads under **Web Page Content**.

## Focus on organic first

- After the site is up, prioritize **organic SEM** (unpaid search listings for relevant content).
- Example searches: lightweight bike, carbon fiber bike → engines rank most relevant sites.
- **Keywords** must be embedded so engines find you. Agency supplies **best keyword set per segment** (not the same as ad benefit bullets).
- Organic is economical but **slow**: engines and biking/fitness sites need time to discover and link; early ad copy will be imperfect. **Do not expect much demand from the initial site alone.**
- Near-term lift: traditional ads that promote brands **and list web URLs** (direct navigation + helps engines find pages).
- **Paid SEM** (buying top-of-page placement) comes later.

**For now:** set up the website and add the right segment keyword sets.

## Setup steps (Workspace)

1. Designate which **ads** convert into web pages.  
2. Select the **segment** each page targets (keywords follow that segment).  
3. Initiate creation (checkmark = run / deploy the page).

### Ad and segment selection

- New ads start **Inactive**.
- Assign segment under **Selection of Segment and Keywords**.
- After assignment, preview web content for a searcher.

### Keywords

- Choosing a segment also chooses that segment’s **recommended keywords**.
- Keywords ≠ ad benefits — they are terms people type when looking for CF / lightweight bikes; each segment has its own set.
- Agency research already selected the best sets; you do not invent keywords.

### Initiate web page creation

- Checkmark signals designers to build the page from the ad outline (benefits + ranking).
- Keywords go into **page content** and **meta tags**.
- Pages are tailored to markets with a sales outlet: **local language + country designation**.

### Costs / deactivate

- Modest fee per web page (confirm $ in Workspace).
- Uncheck to deactivate — page leaves the internet.

## Search Results Page tip (critical for Ad Design)

- Engines show only the **first three benefits** + a link on the SERP.
- Click-through depends on those top three being **highly important** to the target.
- Full benefit list appears only after the click.
- Rank ad benefits with SERP visibility in mind (ties to ≤9 benefits / rank order in Ad Design).

## Deceptive advertising

- Untrue claims are unethical and may trigger **competitor lawsuits**.
- Review Workspace for deceptive flags; fix before running.
- Help file: Deceptive Advertising.
- WeBike / Hike Bike: do not claim lights, reflectors, comfort seat, racing tires, etc., that the bike does not have (see `advertising-background.md`).
