## Philosophy

Several tactics can raise the chance that a **visitor who is already on your site** buys. Industry reports claim they help; **your cohort results are unknown** until you try them.

- Selected tactics run **this quarter**.  
- Full effectiveness takes **a few quarters**.  
- Set a **quarterly budget** per tactic — more spend → better potential quality/quantity, with **diminishing returns**.  
- You can **stop** a service anytime.  
- Help: Web Sales Center.

## Potential improvement curve (Workspace chart)

Both tactics show **steep early gains, then flatten** (diminishing returns). Approximate read-offs:

| Quarterly budget | Toll-free phone (approx %) | Page upgrades (approx %) |
|-----------------:|---------------------------:|-------------------------:|
| $0 | 0 | 0 |
| ~$3,000 | ~30–32 | ~15–16 |
| $5,000 | ~35 | ~18–19 |
| $10,000 | ~41–42 | ~23–24 |
| $20,000 | ~45 | ~27–28 |
| $30,000+ | ~46–47 | ~29–30 |

**Planning takeaway:** Toll-free delivers **higher improvement per dollar** at every budget level on the chart. Most of the gain for both is earned by ~$10k; going $10k→$30k adds only ~5 points. Default budgets ($3k / $6k) sit on the **steep part** of the curve — efficient for a cash-constrained test market if you Start them.

Exact Workspace numbers may differ slightly from chart read-offs — re-check the live graph when locking spend.
