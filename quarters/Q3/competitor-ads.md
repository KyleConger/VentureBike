## Other patterns worth keeping

- **Bike Bros runs 5 ads** (two each for MACH I.I and TERRAMAX, plus Easy Rider) and finished last. Ad count is not the lever.
- **BB LLC advertises only Blu Ruged Ballz + Blu Tube Ballz.** Blu Tail and Blu Aero have no ad. Leader = 2 ads for 4 brands.
- **LiteCycle never claims local sales & service** (44% web). We are 81% store and also never claim it — that is a mismatch on Swift Bike, less urgent on HikeBike 1.
- **“Highest rated” claims are loose.** Armstrong (brand 76) claims highest-rated Speed while MACH I.I and LiteSpeed Pro+ sit at 77. Mars Rover (73 Rec) claims highest-rated Rec while MountainCruise1 sits at 76. BB’s Mountain claim is actually a tie at 73. After Swift Bike’s design hits 77 we *can* claim it as a tie; not needed for the first rebuild.
- Featured Bike row is blank on every ad. Everyone uses “Mention brand name,” not a bike thumbnail.
