## Lessons carried into Q4

1. Scheduled operating capacity (~8/day) was far below owned fixed capacity (24/day) — the stock-out was self-inflicted.
2. Cutting web staff/spend did not help; the constraint was production, not demand generation.
3. Compensation locked at Q1-era levels fell behind peers → productivity 70% / 72% vs 85% projected.
4. Idle cash + repeated stock issues are crushing Asset Management and Wealth.
